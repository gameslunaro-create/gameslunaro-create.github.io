Our game

SkyStrike Arena

Jetpack army shooter

Free on Google Play · Android

Lead Captain Arjun Rana, call sign SHIKRA, and the Sky Strike Force against Black Hydra, a ruthless mercenary army. Fly, slide, dive and fight across hand-drawn battlefields with your squad.

  • 11 story missions in 3 acts, from the Himalayan frontier to the Meghvan jungle and the IRONCLAD boss.
  • 4 Special Ops and Sniper Ops, including a night surgical strike through night vision.
  • Your soldier, your way: 19 uniforms and the Garud Armory's weapons, finishes and attachments.
  • Multiplayer on the same Wi-Fi or online with a room code; the story plays offline.

The studio

About Lunaro

Lunaro is a small independent studio making action games for phones. We care about three things: controls that feel good on a touch screen, hand-drawn worlds worth exploring, and updates that keep coming.

SkyStrike Arena is a work of fiction: every place, unit and enemy in it is invented.
